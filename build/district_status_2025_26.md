# Bourbon County's status scores in the statewide distribution, by level

Status is the year's own result on each accountability indicator, before the state adds its change component. Every Kentucky district's status at the elementary, middle and high school level comes from the district rows of KDE's accountability profile files (KYRC25_ACCT_Profile.csv for 2024-25; Accountable_Profile_2026.CSV, released October 14, 2026, for 2025-26). Rank 1 is the highest status in the state; the percentile is the share of districts at or below Bourbon County, so a low percentile means most districts score higher. The state's level for each score (very low to very high) and the overall color are the ones printed in the state file. The education cooperatives are excluded; the state row is shown for reference.

The status-only overall applies KDE's published indicator weights (elementary 51/40/5/4, middle 46/45/5/4, high 45/20/5/4/20/6 for reading and mathematics, science and social studies and writing, English learner progress, climate and safety, postsecondary readiness and graduation) to status alone, redistributing the weight of any missing indicator proportionally. Weight check: the same weights applied to KDE's combined indicator rates reproduce the published overall score for elementary 173 districts within 0.05; middle 173 districts within 0.05; high 169 districts within 0.05.
