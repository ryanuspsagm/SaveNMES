The state released this year's school scores on October 14. Here is how the county's three elementary schools did, subject by subject, last year against this year, on the score Kentucky builds its ratings from.

North Middletown is lowest in the county in every subject this year, tied with Bourbon Central in science. A year ago it was first in every subject.

Where the big moves are: NMES's reading score fell 22 points, social studies 25, and writing 52. Nothing else in the county moved anywhere near that far. The two largest drops, social studies and writing, are in the two subjects the state tests in a single grade, so they rest on one class. Math barely moved at any school.

Bourbon Central and Cane Ridge were mostly flat: a few points up in reading and science, Cane Ridge up 14 in social studies, Bourbon Central down 11 in writing. Every county school is still below the Kentucky average in every subject.

Kentucky has named NMES a Comprehensive Support and Improvement school on the back of this year. That label means a state audit, a three-year improvement plan and federal improvement money. It is not a closure order.

The state's files, and every number on this chart, are at savenmes.org.
