# Postsecondary readiness, Bourbon County High against Kentucky, 2021-22 to 2025-26

From KDE's postsecondary readiness files, district and state rows, all students. The postsecondary rate is the share of seniors (graduates and grade 12 non-graduates) who met at least one academic or career readiness measure. The rate with bonus is the indicator status the state scores: a senior who holds an industry certification, licensure or credential in a state or regional high-demand sector counts as 1.25 points instead of 1 (703 KAR 5:270, Section 4), so the status can exceed 100. Bourbon County has one high school, so its district row is Bourbon County High School. Rank is among Kentucky districts with a high school result, 1 = highest.
