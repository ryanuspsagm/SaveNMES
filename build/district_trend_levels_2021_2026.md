# Bourbon County by level, every year of the current accountability system

Kentucky's current accountability system began in 2021-22, so the overall score exists for five years. The state issued no overall score in 2016-17, 2017-18, 2019-20 or 2020-21, and only a star rating in 2018-19; the Unbridled Learning scores of 2011-12 to 2015-16 are on a different scale and KDE no longer posts those files. District rows, all students, from KDE's accountability profile files (accountability_profile_2022 and 2023, KYRC24 and KYRC25, Accountable_Profile_2026). In 2021-22 the system scored status only, so that year's overall score has no change component. Ranks are among Kentucky districts with a result at that level, 1 = highest. The last table adds 2020-21, a year with test results but no accountability; its level figures are the grade results weighted by tested students.
