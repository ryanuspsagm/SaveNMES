# Benchmarks from the 2008 to 2011 era, against the current system

The Commonwealth Accountability Testing System (CATS) index that Kentucky published from 1999 to 2011 is no longer posted by KDE; the department supplies those school and district files by data request, and the Wayback Machine is not reachable from this environment. Three public sources that do survive give the district's standing in that era: the Legislative Research Commission's Kentucky District Data Profiles for school years 2008 to 2011 (Core Content Test results by level against the state, the 2008 academic index, ACT, AP and graduation), the federal EDFacts district proficiency series from 2009, and the KYSTATS High School Feedback Reports for Bourbon County High School from the class of 2010. Values from the profiles and feedback reports were transcribed by hand from the PDFs listed under Sources.

The 2008 profile also prints the CATS academic index by subject and level, district (state): reading elementary 100 (95), middle 87 (92), high 81 (88); mathematics 107 (97), 77 (86), 69 (69); science 110 (98), 80 (88), 80 (72); writing 95 (90), 65 (81), 75 (76). The index ran 0 to 140 with 100 as the state's long-term goal.
