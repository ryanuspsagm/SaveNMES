# District year-over-year change, 2024-25 to 2025-26, by level

Every Kentucky district's accountability result at the elementary, middle and high school level, 2024-25 against 2025-26, from the district rows of KDE's two accountability profile files (KYRC25_ACCT_Profile.csv, KDE historical datasets; Accountable_Profile_2026.CSV, released October 14, 2026). Districts are matched on the district number. The education cooperatives and the state row are excluded from the distributions; the state's own change is shown for reference. Status is the year's own result on an indicator; the overall score is the state's published figure, which adds its change component to status. Rank 1 is the largest fall. The percentile is the share of districts at or below the district's change, so a low percentile means one of the larger falls.

Data check: 26 district rows where KDE's published change differs from the difference of the two years' status by more than 0.15. They are listed at the end.
